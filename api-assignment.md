API Needs
CampusCore - Billing/POS

Format: `[Consumer] needs to [verb] [resource] in order to [reason].`

1. CampusCore needs to retrieve the whole list of products in order to list them in its app.
2. CampusCore needs to retrieve specific product information in order to show more info when a buyer clicks on a product.
3. CampusCore needs to retrieve product trends in order to display the most trending products.
4. CampusCore needs to retrieve products bought together in order to provide recommendations.
5. CampusCore needs to set the discount amount per item in order to manage promotions on its platform.
6. CampusCore needs to record a view per product in order to feed product market trend data.
